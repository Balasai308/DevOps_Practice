## 🏍️ **Suzuki Hayabusa**

The **Hayabusa** is a famous high-performance sport bike made by **Suzuki**. It is known for extreme speed, power, and smooth performance.

---

## 🔥 Key Highlights

* **Engine:** 1340cc inline 4-cylinder
* **Top Speed:** Around 299 km/h (electronically limited)
* **Power:** ~190 PS (latest generation)
* **Gearbox:** 6-speed manual
* **Type:** Sport / Hyperbike

---

## 🚀 Why It Is Famous?

* One of the **fastest production bikes** in the world
* Very stable at high speeds
* Strong acceleration
* Popular for racing and modifications
* Very comfortable compared to other superbikes

---

## 🧠 Name Meaning

“Hayabusa” means **Peregrine Falcon** in Japanese —
a bird known for being the fastest in the world.

It Once Broke the Speed War

In the late 1990s, Hayabusa became the fastest production motorcycle in the world (around 312 km/h).

After that, manufacturers like Suzuki, Honda, and Kawasaki agreed to limit top speeds to 299 km/h.

That’s why modern superbikes are electronically limited.
King of Modifications

Hayabusa is one of the most modified bikes in the world:

Turbocharged builds

400+ HP drag race versions

Extended swingarms

Custom paint & stretched models

In drag racing, modified Hayabusas can cross 400 km/h

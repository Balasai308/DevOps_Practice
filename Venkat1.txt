## 🎬 Oppenheimer – Story Summary
**Director:** Christopher Nolan
**Based on:** American Prometheus
---
### 🔬 Early Life & Career
The movie tells the story of **J. Robert Oppenheimer**, a brilliant physicist.
He studies physics in Europe and becomes an important scientist in the United States.
He later becomes known as the “Father of the Atomic Bomb.”
---
### 💣 The Manhattan Project
During **World War II**, the U.S. government starts a secret project called the **Manhattan Project**.
Oppenheimer is chosen to lead the project at **Los Alamos, New Mexico**.
The goal is to build the first atomic bomb before Nazi Germany.
Many top scientists work under him.
---
### ⚡ First Atomic Bomb Test
In 1945, they successfully test the first atomic bomb.
This test is called **Trinity Test**.
Oppenheimer famously remembers a line from the **Bhagavad Gita**:
> “Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds.”
---
### 🌍 Hiroshima & Nagasaki
The U.S. drops atomic bombs on:
* Hiroshima
* Nagasaki
Japan surrenders, and World War II ends.
But thousands of people die.
Oppenheimer feels guilt and emotional conflict.
---
### 🏛 Political Trouble
After the war, Oppenheimer opposes the hydrogen bomb.
During the Cold War, he is accused of having communist connections.
The U.S. government removes his security clearance.
His reputation is damaged.
---
### 🎭 Movie Theme
The movie focuses on:
* Science vs morality
* Power and responsibility
* Politics and betrayal
* Psychological struggle
It shows how one man changed the world — and suffered because of it.
